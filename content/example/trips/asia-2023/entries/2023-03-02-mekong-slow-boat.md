Two days on a wooden boat with a car engine bolted into the back of it. There is nothing to do, which is the entire point and takes about four hours to understand.

We filmed ten seconds of the bank going past on the second afternoon. It is the only video either of us took the whole trip and it is somehow the thing we show people first.
