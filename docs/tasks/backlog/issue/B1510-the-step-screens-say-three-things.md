# B1510 — The step screens say three things the screen already says

## Why

Three lines the owner reads past, all of them saying what the screen has
already said:

- **`1 von 3` under the step bar.** There are three pills, one of them is
  filled, and it is the first. The count restates the bar directly beneath it.
- **`Das Foto steht fest — ein anderes bedeutet eine neue Karte.`** on the Look
  step. Nothing on the screen offers another photograph, so this answers a
  question nobody asked, under the control that does the one thing you can do.
- **The second half of the with-the-printer note** (B1499): *"wann sie
  ankommen, erfährt dieses Journal nicht, und darum behauptet es hier auch
  nichts."* Honest, and too much — the first sentence already says the printer
  has it and the cards go out over the following days, which is the whole of
  what a person needs.

And one thing in the wrong order: **`Die Vorderseite` sits under the
photograph**, labelling it after it has been looked at. It belongs above, the
way the card of rows on the Write step names itself before its contents.

## Work

Remove the three. Move the front's label above the frame. The drag-and-slider
hint stays — it is the only line on that step that says what to do.

The removed strings go from all three locales where nothing else reads them.

## Acceptance

At 390: no count under the pills, no fixed-photograph sentence, the front
labelled above its own picture, and the printer note one sentence long.
