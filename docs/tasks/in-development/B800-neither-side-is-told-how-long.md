# B800 — Neither side is told how long an approval usually takes

## Why

Mum is told *"You'll get an email the moment they wave you in."* The traveller
gets a mail saying somebody is waiting. Neither is told **when**.

So Mum cannot tell "not yet approved" from "broken", and if she reopens the day
URL while waiting she meets the same generic sign-in gate with no "you are
still waiting" — the pending state only shows on the tab she left open. The
traveller has no sense that anybody is blocked on her.

Found while playing both sides on the live instance.

## Work

A rough expectation in both places — "most people are let in within a day or
two" — and a pending reader who returns to the gate should be told they are
already in the queue rather than shown the join form again.

## Acceptance

A waiting reader who comes back to the link learns they are waiting.
