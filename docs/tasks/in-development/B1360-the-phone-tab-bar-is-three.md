# B1360 — The phone tab bar is three bare words with a dash of yellow

## Why

The phone tab bar was three bare words with a thin yellow dash — too
light for an app bar (owner, F02 A).

## Work

Each tab carries an icon (chat bubble, folder, eye) beside its word; the
active tab is a filled yellow pill; the bar grew to min-h-14. "Wie es
aussieht" renamed to "Vorschau"/"Preview"/"Előnézet" (the same key names
the desktop pane, which reads better shorter too).

## Acceptance

At 390px the active tab renders as a yellow pill with icon and word, the
inactive tabs as quiet icon-and-word; the preview dot still appears when a
day arrives on another tab.
