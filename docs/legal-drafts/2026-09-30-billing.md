# Draft: terms and privacy changes for the three plans (B2596)

For the owner's lawyer only. Nothing here is live legal text — the site's
own `/legal` page and its template are unchanged by this ticket. This is a
list of the facts the terms and privacy notice need to cover now that Free,
Trip pass and Plus actually charge (`docs/billing.md` has the full, decided
rules; this file only points a lawyer at what needs new or changed wording).

## The three plans, in one line each

- **Free**, CHF 0. Never charges. AI tools for 10 travel days, once per
  owner for life.
- **Trip pass**, CHF 19 once (CHF 22 inside the iPhone app), a single
  non-recurring charge. Runs 45 days from purchase; AI tools for 21 of those
  days.
- **Plus**, CHF 49 a year (CHF 59 inside the app), a recurring annual
  subscription. AI tools for 100 travel days per subscription year.

Prices in Swiss francs; Stripe on the web, Apple in the iPhone app
(in-app purchase, a later ticket).

## Renewal and the reminder

Plus renews automatically every year on the anniversary of purchase unless
cancelled first. The owner gets an email reminder **30 days before** the
renewal charge. A failed renewal charge gets a **7-day grace period**
(the plan's limits still apply) before it actually ends.

Needs terms language on: automatic renewal, the right to cancel before the
next charge, and what "grace period" means for the owner (not a promise of
extra service, a short buffer before the plan actually lapses).

## Cancellation

Cancelling Plus is two taps in the owner's own account page, through
Stripe's Customer Portal. Cancelling stops the *next* charge; the plan the
owner already paid for keeps running to the end of its paid period — nothing
is refunded pro rata, and nothing is cut off early.

Needs a plain statement of this "runs to period end, no partial refund"
policy, since it's the one place a customer might expect an immediate
stop or a refund.

## What happens when a plan ends

Whether by cancellation, a pass's 45 days running out, or a grace period
being exhausted:

- AI tools (photo descriptions, dictation, polish) stop working.
- Nothing already written, photographed or published is deleted.
- The journal's content stays readable and printable at the Free plan's
  terms.
- No new upload above the Free plan's 2 GB quota is accepted (existing
  content over quota is never touched or removed).

Needs terms language making clear that "the plan ending" never means data
loss — this is a downgrade to Free's limits, not an account closure.

## Payment processors

- **Stripe** processes every web payment (card, TWINT) and holds the
  card/payment details; Fernscout's own servers never see or store them.
  Stripe issues the receipt for a printed postcard or photobook order too.
- **Apple** processes every in-app purchase made inside the iPhone app
  (Trip pass and Plus bought there cost more — CHF 22 / CHF 59 — because
  Apple takes its own commission); Apple's own terms and refund policy
  govern a purchase made through the App Store, not Fernscout's.

Needs both named as payment processors/sub-processors in the privacy notice
(what of the owner's data reaches them — email and name for the Stripe
checkout session; nothing beyond what Apple's own StoreKit already holds
for an App Store purchase) and in the terms (which processor's terms govern
a given purchase, depending on where it was made).

## EU/EEA consent to start at once

Because a plan (a "digital service") starts immediately (AI tools unlock
the moment payment succeeds), an EU/EEA buyer must expressly consent to
losing their 14-day right of withdrawal by starting the service straight
away, before paying. This consent needs to be captured (a checkbox or an
equivalent explicit step) before Stripe checkout begins, and the terms need
the corresponding withdrawal-waiver clause. This is not yet built in the
checkout flow (`app/api/web/[user]/billing/checkout/route.ts`) — flagging it
here since the terms language and the checkout step depend on each other;
whoever adds the checkbox should point back to this paragraph.

## Upgrade coupon

A Trip pass owner who upgrades to Plus within 60 days gets a single-use
Stripe coupon crediting the CHF 19 already paid. Worth a line in the terms
so "why does Plus cost less this once" has a documented answer if a buyer
asks.

## No credits, no WhatsApp postcards

The old pay-as-you-go credit balance and the WhatsApp postcard are gone —
nothing in the new terms should still describe a credit purchase, a credit
balance, or a WhatsApp notification/postcard as something sold. Printed
postcards and photobooks are still sold, paid at checkout, no balance
involved.
