Concept 1 of 3 · WhatsApp

The Day Funnel

WhatsApp stops being an assistant that can do anything and becomes one thing done perfectly: today's day, captured and published. Everything else is named, and handed to a page.

Draft concept 16 Sept 2026 Compliance-forced

The model didn't fail. It was handed forty ways to be wrong and asked to pick one.

40 tools across 7 areas, offered on every inbound message, with a switch_area tool that can widen back to all of them
6 tools: resolve the day, stage files, read state, draft words, polish, publish. Most states run no model at all

The corollary nobody enjoys: most of this funnel is deterministic. Day resolution, folder opening, the mechanical draft, the redirects, the nudge — no LLM, no credits, no ambiguity. The model is left with the one job it is genuinely good at: turning a spoken note into prose, when asked and paid for.

Not a preference

Four constraints that decide the design

Three are Meta's, dated. One is arithmetic. None are negotiable, and together they draw the flow almost without us.

15 Jan 2026 · in force

General-purpose bots are banned

Meta prohibits open-domain AI on the Business Platform. AI is allowed only where it is "ancillary to a legitimate business service, not the centerpiece" — and a handoff path to another channel is required. The current agent is on the wrong side of this line. The redirects are the required handoff.

Always

The 24-hour window

Free-form replies only within 24h of his last message; every inbound resets it. Outside, only an approved template. So the nudge lands at +20h or never. Marketing templates can't rescue it — capped at 2/user/day across all businesses, and paused for US numbers since April 2025.

1 Oct 2026 · six weeks out

Questions start costing money

In-window service messages become billable. "Ask at most one thing per turn, at most twice" stops being a UX preference and becomes a line item. Every gap you can derive from EXIF is a question you never pay for.

Arithmetic

Three buttons, twenty characters

Reply buttons cap at 3, titles at 20 chars. A fourth option forces a list message, which reads as a form. Veröffentlichen fits at 15; Jetzt veröffentlichen at 21 does not. That is a test across all three locales, not a hope.

The flow

One trail, with marked exits

Waymarks are states the person passes through. Hollow marks are the only two places the funnel asks him anything. Grey offshoots leave the trail for a page — and nothing is ever dropped on the way out.

S1

Something arrives

Photo, voice note, text, or a location pin. Contact cards, documents and videos leave immediately — they have their own pages now.

No model
S2

Which day this is

Derived, not asked. Photo EXIF date wins when all photos agree. Otherwise the message timestamp in the journal's timezone — with 00:00–03:59 meaning yesterday, because someone writing up at 01:30 means the day that just ended. getCurrentTrip() already derives the trip from its date range; no new resolver needed.

No model
Exit · no trip, or older than 3 days

Files stay in the undated inbox, nothing lost, and he gets the link to the page that assigns them. The 3-day cutoff covers the flight home plus one jetlagged evening; past that the job is backfill, which needs a calendar.

S4 · asks

Only when the date is genuinely unclear

Three buttons: Today / Yesterday / Another day. Never four. "Another day" is an exit, not a fourth branch.

No model
S3

The day folder opens

inbox/days/<date>/ — already built, already has moveInboxFileToDay(). Re-entering with more photos just adds them. Idempotent by construction.

No model
S5

The mechanical draft

Gallery sorted by EXIF time, cover = first photo, coordinates from EXIF or the pin, transcript and text verbatim in arrival order. Title empty unless he gave one. An empty title beats an invented one. This costs nothing and is fully publishable.

Free · no model
S6 · asks

At most one gap, at most twice

In order: location, if no photo has GPS and no pin arrived. Then whether anything happened at all, if there's no text and no voice. Title is folded into the confirmation, never its own turn. Costs, transport, weather, tags and people are never asked here — they are web fields, and every extra ask was a way to be wrong.

No model
S8

The draft link, always

Sent whatever happens, with a one-line inventory of what actually went in: "12 photos, a 40-second note, no location." This is simultaneously the escape hatch Meta requires and the one NN/g recommends.

Free
S9

The polish offer — priced before the spend

Writing prose from the transcript, and describing photographs. Offered once per day folder, with the number stated first. Short on credits? He is told, and the free draft still publishes. The free version is never a hostage.

Credits · the only paid step
S13

One nudge, at +20h

Inside his own window so it is free-form and free, and only if a gap is open and the draft unpublished. Window closed — send nothing. He has the link; the page holds the state. It says so out loud: "That's the only reminder I'll send."

Free
S10

Publish — owner, explicit, in words

The bot restates what is about to go live, then offers the button, never in the same message. A thumbs-up is not consent. Silence is not consent. "Looks good" is not consent. Afterwards it reports what the call returned — never "published!" because it was asked for.

Free

Already works: publishing from WhatsApp resolves through isHelperOwner on the v1 helper route, which accepts a WhatsApp caller. The v2 route is unreachable from chat — no path connects a trusted caller to a bearer session. Leave it on v1; moving it means touching mayActAsOwner, which is a security-review gate.

Seen from his phone

Eight scenarios, four worth looking at

Tone rules held throughout: state only what arrived, name what is missing rather than filling it, one ask per turn. No exclamation marks. No "Great!". No invented place, meal, weather or feeling.

The mechanism

The folder is the record. The chat is a cache.

The 24-hour thread TTL stops being a problem the moment conversational memory is no longer where state lives. A dropped thread loses turns; it loses nothing that matters. The next message resolves to the same date, opens the same folder, reads the same file, and carries on mid-sentence.

Three fields on the existing day.json — no new file format, and the merge-patch writer already in lib/dayReadiness.ts takes them unchanged.

  • stage — so a reconnecting funnel knows whether it is collecting, drafted, or waiting on a publish confirmation
  • asked — so it never re-asks a question he answered three messages and one expiry ago
  • draftWords — today draft_words writes nothing, so a draft dies with the thread and the credits are spent for nothing

One caution from the build map: shrinking the WhatsApp tool list to six will likely drop the prompt under Haiku's 4,096-token cache floor, silently losing the caching saving. The readers area already sits at ~3,990, under the line. Worth measuring, not discovering.

Before building

Still open

  • Is 3 days the right tail? It covers the flight home plus one jetlagged evening. Longer, and a stray photo silently attaches itself to a finished trip.
  • Does the funnel ever ask for a title? Currently folded into the confirmation. The alternative is never asking and letting the page handle it.
  • What happens to the other six tool areas on WhatsApp? Redirect is the plan — but switch_area can currently widen back to everything, so the restriction has to bite in runTool, not just in what the model is offered.
  • Hungarian. Every string here needs a real hu entry. Nobody on this has working Hungarian, and the repo rule forbids inventing one — this is a hand-off, not a to-do.
  • Utility template for the closed-window case? Currently: send nothing. An approved utility template would allow one late nudge, at the cost of an approval cycle and a real per-message fee.
