Three Doors

The studio, your own agent, and WhatsApp. Two of them reach everything; the third is for the evening in a tent. The hosted chat is not one of them.

The API is a front door, not a convenience

"Bring your own agent" only means something if the v2 contract genuinely covers what the studio covers. A flow and an endpoint are two faces of one capability. Every studio flow reaching something the API cannot reach is a broken promise — and keep-the-contract is the check that catches it.

Why the hosted chat is not a door

/agent tried to be doors one and two at once: a chat that does the work itself, hosted by us, on our credits, against a model we picked. But the person who wants an agent mostly already has one. It retires into the other two — actions become flows, agency becomes theirs.

Door one

Every action wears the same five steps

Generalised from the import shape in concept 2, which turned out to be a general answer wearing an import costume. Not every flow needs all five; every flow uses them in this order.

1

What this is

One sentence on what you get, and any promise that must be made at the moment of asking.

2

Gather

The questions, or the file. One thing per screen.

3

Preview

What will happen, in real content. Says out loud that nothing has been written.

4

Decide

Check answers. Every row changeable, every choice visible.

5

Do it

One button named after its consequence. Never Next, never Save.

  • Nothing is written before step 5. A person can back out anywhere and the journal is untouched
  • A flow can be left and resumed — the photographs flow already does this; it becomes part of the skeleton
  • Steps are self-sufficient — no step needs information from elsewhere in the app. This is the rule that deletes the documentation
  • Publishing is always its own decision, never a side effect of a flow finishing

What this revises

Yesterday's plan had the split the wrong way round

Concepts 1–3 assumed WhatsApp owns day creation and the browser mops up. The narrowing was right; the split was not. The funnel still gets built — it is no longer the primary path.

  • B1820 keeps its design, loses its primacy. Its redirects now point into studio flows — deep into a flow, not at the hub
  • B1821 becomes the New trip flow
  • B1822, B1823 become Bring in flows
  • B1824 is where the flow skeleton is built. No longer import-specific — the hub consumes it. These two should be taken together
  • B1825 changes target: /extract → /studio, not /import. Its collision with B1803 cleared when B1803 merged
  • B1826 unchanged — and /docs/helper matters more now, because it is door two's front page
Order

1

The skeleton and the hub

The flow component, resume, preview and decide, and /studio listing them. Nothing else can be a flow until this exists.

2

The main flow, and the first proof the skeleton works for something that is not a file upload.

3

The existing work, rehoused

Edit a day, new trip, invite, the imports — wearing the skeleton, appearing in the hub.

Move, split, merge

The remaining genuine gap.

5

The rename

Whenever convenient after the hub exists, since it now points at /studio.

6

The WhatsApp funnel

Last, because its redirects need flows to redirect into.
